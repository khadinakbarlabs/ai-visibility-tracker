# Coordinate SEO and AI visibility research

Use this as the entry point for a complete campaign. These are specialist skill roles executed by the host, not bundled autonomous processes. Route them sequentially by default; host subagents are optional and must share the same budget ledger.

1. Establish the domain, business/category, audience, country, language, device, known competitors, existing exports, output folder and total spend cap. Ask only for missing essentials. Resolve country names, country codes and DataForSEO location codes explicitly; never reuse one format in another field.
2. Inventory existing evidence. Propose run counts, current pricing estimates, allocations and stop conditions. An unapproved budget permits planning and saved-export analysis only. Read [execution](references/execution.md) and [campaign state](references/campaign-state.md).
3. Route competitor discovery and comparable traffic/Google checks to `competitor-intelligence`; topic demand to `keyword-opportunities`; seasonality to `search-trends`. Distinguish businesses from publishers, forums and directories before selecting competitors.
4. Fix a small Google keyword panel and a separate literal AI question panel. Route citations to `ai-visibility-tracker` and saved AI comparisons to `visibility-trends`. Record the market, device, models, questions and dates. Do not conflate Google rank with AI citation position.
5. Route capped own/competitor backlink samples to `competitor-backlinks`, then feed their evidence plus citation URLs to `link-opportunities`. Link discovery and cited-source overlap identify prospects, not guaranteed authority or citation gains.
6. Route all usable evidence to `visibility-action-plan`. Return one report: baseline coverage, competitor table, keyword/trend findings, backlink gaps, verified/provisional prospects, prioritized action queue, run costs and missing evidence. Avoid duplicating raw specialist reports.
7. Save panels and action IDs outside the installed plugin. On return, resume known runs and repeat matching panels. Do not create a recurring schedule unless the user separately authorizes cadence, spend and notifications and a durable scheduler is available and verified.

For partial budgets, prioritize the user's question, reuse existing results and omit lower-priority runs explicitly. Never claim all modules were measured when only some ran.
