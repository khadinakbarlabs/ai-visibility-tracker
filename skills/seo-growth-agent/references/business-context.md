# Private business context

Maintain a compact current profile per website: offering, audience/use cases, accepted business goal, priority pages, relevant competitors, markets, constraints, preferences, corrections and pending decisions. Store privately, not in plugin files or a publisher service. Retrieve relevant evidence on demand; don't load entire histories or transcripts every session.

Each factual claim records claim ID, website/market scope, value, provenance kind (user supplied, publicly verified, inferred, measured), source/evidence IDs, observedAt, reviewedAt and status. Confidence includes a rationale, not an invented probability. Inferences remain labelled even when used as defaults. Preferences change decisions but cannot rewrite measurements. A correction supersedes a claim while preserving history and changes only its intended scope.

Check freshness for the decision. A recently read product page can support offering; an old SERP cannot establish today's rank. Save a suitable review date per claim/evidence type where possible; refresh when staleness could change the decision, within authorization. Don't impose universal expiration or launch paid refreshes automatically. Archived evidence remains valid for dated historical observations.

On return load the goal/profile, compatible findings, pending runs, action statuses, preferences, remaining budget, pauses and next due work. Ask only if identity or a consequential decision remains ambiguous. Never infer private conversion rates, analytics, team capacity or market targeting from public copy/timezone.

Optional first-party enrichment uses an existing authorized Search Console tool/export for clicks, impressions, CTR and average position, or authorized analytics/conversion exports for outcomes. Preserve property, date range, filters, aggregation and provenance. Average position differs from sampled SERP position; no conversion/revenue estimates without evidence. This skill adds no Google connection, scopes or account access. Missing access leaves enrichment unavailable.

Support “show what you know”, “correct this”, “forget this preference”, “export my campaign” and “delete my campaign”. Explain affected local records/external resources; pause tracking before removing state needed by jobs. Use appropriate authorized deletion tools and verify readback; disclose inaccessible exports/cloud retention. Deleting a summary doesn't delete host history or Apify records. Removing a preference leaves historical measurements intact.
