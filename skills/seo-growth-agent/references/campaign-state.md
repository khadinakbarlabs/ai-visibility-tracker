# Campaign continuity

Use a private user-selected folder outside the plugin. Persist a concise project record: domain/business/audience; market/language/device; confirmed competitor classifications; fixed Google keywords; literal AI questions and platforms; trend geo/timeframe/property; backlink sampling filters; and dates.

Maintain a budget ledger with authorized total, per-run allocation/reservation, Actor/run/build IDs, status, observed charges, unresolved charges and remaining available allocation. An unresolved run holds its reservation. Do not reset the budget when switching skills or resuming a chat.

Maintain an evidence index for saved inputs, dataset exports, summaries and timestamps; an action ledger with action ID, supporting observations, owner, priority, suggested work and next matching measurement. Raw credentials, account metadata and private customer data do not belong in campaign records. Avoid overwriting earlier panels/results. A user-provided export lacking provenance can support qualified observations, not a verified historical comparison.

No automatic monitoring exists in this package. Recurrence requires separately authorized cadence, budget and notification rules plus a verified durable scheduler. Store its ID only after confirmation. Compare matching windows and panels; observed changes do not establish causation.
