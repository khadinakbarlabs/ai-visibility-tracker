# Research keyword opportunities

Use `khadinakbar/dataforseo-keyword-research`. Select `keyword_ideas` for expansion or `search_volume` for a known list. Confirm market and language; use a verified DataForSEO locationCode, not an inferred country-code conversion. Cap seeds and result count before estimating costs.

Cluster results by buyer intent, topic and relevant landing page. Preserve observed searchVolume, monthlySearches, cpcUsd and returned difficulty/intent fields with provenance. Google Ads advertiser competition is not SEO difficulty. Fields absent in the selected mode remain unknown; do not invent them.

Prioritize business relevance, actual demand, existing page coverage and observed competitor visibility. CPC indicates advertiser demand, not guaranteed revenue. Proposed AI questions are hypotheses until measured by the citation skill. Return a small ranked topic set, supporting metrics, proposed Google keywords and separate buyer questions for the lead workflow.
